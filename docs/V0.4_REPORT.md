# V0.4 — Temperate Wilds Terrain

V0.4 keeps the V0.3 Rabbit/Fox simulation and eight-checkpoint progression intact while making land cover, refuge distance, and Stream crossings affect placement and ecological outcomes.

## Built

- A single seeded Temperate Wilds terrain model shared by simulation, rendering, placement, ecology, and debug tooling.
- Four recognizable environments: implicit Meadow, continuous Woodland and Thicket cover, and a separate Stream hydrology/barrier layer.
- One coherent maximum-world Stream with natural bends, variable width, deep water, visible banks, and three seeded shallow fords. Expansion reveals the same pre-generated channel.
- Terrain-aware placement, food regeneration, Rabbit refuge choice, Fox pursuit disruption, route-aware food/prey/threat/mating checks, Safe Haven evidence, and dry newborn placement.
- Organic code-drawn terrain art, Stream animation/highlights, stepping-stone fords, Thicket ground and foreground foliage, water-safe decoration, and F3 route/habitat visualization.
- Focused V0.4 automated coverage and reproducible live-simulation terrain playtests.

No animals, foods, hazards, persistence, currencies, or meta-progression were added. `carrot_patch` remains the implementation and player-facing item name.

## Terrain architecture

`TemperateWildsTerrain` in `simulation/temperate_wilds_terrain.gd` is the source of truth.

- **Meadow:** implicit traversable land where neither Woodland nor Thicket cover is strong and Stream water does not override the point. It is sampled as a continuous value, not instantiated as objects or tiles.
- **Woodland:** seeded irregular elliptical descriptors sampled into continuous cover. The generation method preserves the existing forest descriptor layout and seed behavior.
- **Thicket:** a second set of smaller seeded organic descriptors, including several in the initial-radius band and more revealed through expansion. It is queried continuously; there are no shrub colliders.
- **Stream:** a 49-point seeded centerline generated across the maximum 940-unit world, with per-point widths. Water depth is derived from distance to the nearest indexed segment and reduced around fords.
- **Crossings:** three fixed seeded ford descriptors at configured positions along the channel. Each stores a center, tangent, normal, width, and shallow radius. Only fords within the current revealed radius can be used.
- **Reachability:** `route_between` first tests a sampled direct ground segment. If water blocks it, it evaluates visible fords and returns the cheapest valid bank-approach/ford/bank-exit waypoint route. It also returns route distance, direct distance, and the selected ford. This is deliberately specialized for one Stream rather than generic navigation or per-tick A*.

Patch and Stream segment bins bound cover/water work to nearby descriptors. `local_habitat_composition` exposes Meadow/Woodland/Thicket/water mixtures for current food rules and future edge-oriented species without creating a fifth “edge” terrain.

## Rabbit changes

- Ordinary habitat steering retains the existing weak preference for open ground, so Rabbits continue spending meaningful time in Meadow.
- Food selection scores reachable route distance rather than straight-line distance. Static plant routes are retained with the target instead of recalculated every tick.
- Threat evaluation ignores a Fox whose valid route exceeds the flee radius even if its straight-line position is close across water.
- On a serious threat, a Rabbit searches nearby Thicket patches for a reachable refuge that does not move it closer to the Fox. It visibly follows that route when a useful patch exists and otherwise uses the existing flee-away behavior.
- Rabbits retain 96% of normal movement in full Thicket. This makes refuge movement readable without granting a generic standing-stat bonus or invulnerability.
- Mating, local reproduction food, and target food checks require reachable route distance. Birth placement retries and scans for dry traversable ground.

## Fox changes

- Idle/wandering Foxes retain the existing Woodland preference.
- A hungry Fox selects prey by reachable route distance. A route through a ford is cached; moving prey triggers a replan only after the configured displacement threshold.
- Active pursuit can leave Woodland and chase into Meadow; habitat steering remains secondary while hunting.
- Foxes can enter Thicket, but full cover reduces their movement to 74%, capture buildup to 58%, and capture range to 84% of their open-ground values. Hunts still succeed, so Thicket disrupts pursuit without disabling predation.
- Fox mating also uses reachable route distance, and Fox placement/newborn placement rejects deep water.

## Food changes

- **Carrot Patch** is unchanged in name, inventory role, and visual identity. Its regeneration suitability rises with local Meadow composition, from a configured 0.42 floor to a 1.20 cap. Placement in mediocre land remains valid; the patch simply recovers less productively.
- **Berry Bush** favors mixed cover around a configured 0.46 Woodland/Thicket margin. Deep cover is penalized, preventing the best food from also sitting in the strongest refuge.
- Suitability is stored on each plant and affects regeneration, so the player reads the result from visible fullness/recovery rather than modifiers or percentages.

## Progression impact

The checkpoint order, evidence types, thresholds, rewards, supply pools, Critical/Game Over behavior, milestone presentation, sandbox, and restart were not redesigned. The only RunDirector integration is terrain-aware grouping/food reachability and route-based separation for **Two Safe Havens**.

No checkpoint tuning changes were required. In the deliberate live run, all eight checkpoints completed at simulation time 204.8; the longest checkpoint wait was 61.6 seconds. The run included 21 natural births, 7 successful hunts, 2 reachable safe havens, 2 living Foxes, the required ordered final cycle, and successful sandbox continuation. Predator overstock still reached Game Over, while indiscriminate central dumping stalled at Two Safe Havens.

## Navigation

Ground navigation is a terrain-owned narrow interface:

1. Reject endpoints outside the revealed world or in deep water.
2. Sample the direct segment at the configured spacing.
3. If clear, return a direct route.
4. If blocked, evaluate only currently revealed fords.
5. Return the shortest valid approach → ford → exit waypoint sequence inside the caller's maximum route distance.

Animals cache the chosen target, waypoints, route distance, direct distance, and ford. Static food routes do not replan. Moving prey only replans after it has moved 34 units and the 0.55-second replan interval permits it. Waypoints are held until the animal comes within 17 units, preventing bank target-flipping. The movement guard also rejects a deep-water next position and chooses the most useful valid bank tangent/escape direction as a final collision safeguard.

This same reachability meaning is used for Rabbit food and threat selection, Fox prey selection, Rabbit/Fox mating, local food, Safe Haven membership/food/separation, and debug inspection.

Live terrain trial observations:

- **A — Open Meadow:** the colony grew from 6 to 20 before Fox pressure, then ended with 12; 5 successful captures and 2,750 aggregate flee ticks made exposure dangerous.

## Tuning map

- **Woodland generation:** `config/game_config.gd` → `world.forest_patch_count`, `forest_patch_min_radius`, `forest_patch_max_radius`; generation/sampling in `simulation/temperate_wilds_terrain.gd` → `_generate_woodland`, `woodland_cover`.
- **Woodland behavior:** `simulation/ecosystem_simulation.gd` → `_habitat_steering`, `_rabbit_wander_velocity`, `_update_fox`.
- **Thicket generation/effects:** `config/game_config.gd` → `terrain.thicket`; generation/sampling in `simulation/temperate_wilds_terrain.gd` → `_generate_thicket`, `thicket_cover`, `ground_speed_factor`; capture effects in `simulation/ecosystem_simulation.gd` → `_update_fox`.
- **Stream geometry/depth:** `config/game_config.gd` → `terrain.stream`; generation/sampling in `simulation/temperate_wilds_terrain.gd` → `_generate_stream`, `stream_info`, `water_depth`, `is_deep_water`.
- **Crossings:** `config/game_config.gd` → `terrain.stream.ford_positions`, `ford_radius`; route construction in `simulation/temperate_wilds_terrain.gd` → `route_between`, `_ford_strength`.
- **Refuge behavior:** `config/game_config.gd` → `terrain.thicket.refuge_search_radius`, `refuge_cover_min`; selection in `simulation/temperate_wilds_terrain.gd` → `nearest_reachable_thicket`; use in `simulation/ecosystem_simulation.gd` → `_update_rabbit`.
- **Food suitability:** `config/game_config.gd` → `terrain.food_suitability`; formula in `simulation/temperate_wilds_terrain.gd` → `food_suitability`; application in `simulation/ecosystem_simulation.gd` → `add_plant`, `_regenerate_plants`.
- **Routing/cache:** `config/game_config.gd` → `terrain.routing`; terrain queries in `simulation/temperate_wilds_terrain.gd` → `route_between`, `direct_path_clear`; target/waypoint retention in `simulation/ecosystem_simulation.gd` → `_set_ground_route`, `_velocity_along_route`, `_rabbit_food_target_is_valid`.
- **Safe Haven reachability:** `game/run_director.gd` → `spatial_evidence`, `_minimum_group_distance`.
- **Terrain rendering/debug:** `rendering/world_view.gd` → `_draw_terrain`, `_draw_stream`, `_draw_thicket_foreground`, `_draw_debug`; text inspection in `game/main.gd` → `_update_debug_panel`.

## Known issues

No functional issue was found within the V0.4 scope after automated, live-simulation, and real-renderer validation. The navigation model is intentionally specialized to the single fixed Stream and ford set; additional water bodies or terrain mutation would require later navigation work and were explicitly excluded from this version.

## Four playtest questions

1. Without reading debug values, can you correctly infer which patches are Thicket refuge and which pale bands are shallow Stream crossings?
2. Does placing food near Thicket feel meaningfully safer than an exposed Meadow colony while still leaving Fox attacks tense and successful sometimes?
3. Does Stream geography change which bank you establish on and when/where you introduce Foxes, or do crossings feel too easy to ignore?
4. Across a full eight-checkpoint run, do terrain decisions enrich the pacing without making food access, Two Safe Havens, or predator recovery feel arbitrary?
