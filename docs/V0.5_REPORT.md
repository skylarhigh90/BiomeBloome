# Biome Bloome V0.5 compound progression report

## Outcome

V0.5 uses ten meadow beats. Five focused rabbit checkpoints build the colony in deliberate steps before the five-checkpoint predator arc begins.

The progression layer accepts multiple criteria per checkpoint and exposes every live condition to the HUD. Event evidence is fresh to the current checkpoint; living-state evidence must remain true through the configured hold.

## Checkpoint arc

| # | Checkpoint | Compound proof |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | A Colony Gathers | Four living rabbits hold for four seconds. |
| 2 | New Arrivals | Six living rabbits, including two fresh natural births. |
| 3 | Young Foragers | Eight living rabbits, with two meadow-born rabbits mature enough to feed. |
| 4 | Life Across the Meadow | Ten living rabbits and fresh births in three separated areas. |
| 5 | A Nursery Network | Twelve living rabbits and three simultaneous fed groups of at least three rabbits. |
| 6 | Hunt and Recover | A fox kill followed by a rabbit birth, with viable populations. |
| 7 | Predator–Prey Rhythm | Fox kill → rabbit birth → fox kill, with species-specific hunger and quantitative rabbit-loss status. |
| 8 | Havens Under Pressure | Two separated viable refuges survive `hunt → birth` and a 12-second all-goals hold. |
| 9 | Two Hunting Foxes | Three refuges, kill → birth → kill, two distinct living hunters, and four rabbits per fox survive a 14-second hold. |
| 10 | Living Ecosystem | Three refuges, two hunters, births in separated zones, four rabbits per fox, and `hunt → birth → hunt → birth → hunt` survive a 20-second hold. |

Extra births or hunts do not invalidate ordered evidence. An unfinished sequence expires at the configured window; a completed sequence stays latched while the living conditions settle.

## Playtest-led tuning

The opening targets are intentionally cumulative without duplicating the same proof: population, births, young feeding, distributed births, then live nurseries. The 4 → 6 → 8 → 10 → 12 population ramp prevents passive evidence from advancing a checkpoint after losses, while three birth areas remain achievable inside the initial meadow.

The strategy bot was improved instead of weakening the game. It now:

- establishes six founders across three habitats;
- searches for productive terrain before placing plants;
- prioritizes recovery supplies below the rabbit reserve;
- delays a second fox until the ecosystem has rebuilt;
- can spend additional forage inventory during a low-population recovery.

Manual playtesting then exposed a non-monotonic connected-component rule: extra Rabbits could chain healthy nurseries together, and one unfed wanderer could invalidate the resulting oversized component. Safe-haven evidence now recognizes bounded local nursery nuclei with their own forage. The opening network and expanded late-game meadow both support three distinct groups.

The long strategy harness caps rabbits at 16 and foxes at 10 to keep route-aware headless comparisons practical. This does not change the live game configuration; 16 rabbits still exceed the final 12-rabbit target and support the required prey ratio.

Observed deterministic-seed outcomes:

| Strategy | Horizon/outcome | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Dump everything | Stalled at Life Across the Meadow at 360 seconds | 16 rabbits and 27 natural births still produced only one birthplace. Quantity cannot bypass geography. |
| Deliberate refuge-network | Reached Predators Find Their Place at 350.4 seconds; still playing there at 800 | The three-birthplace step took 177.2 seconds, followed by a 25.5-second nursery-network step. The strategy accumulated 70 natural births and 18 hunts without collapsing. |
| Predator overstock | Reached Predators Find Their Place at 487.9 seconds; still playing there at 700 | Aggressive Fox use caused 17 hunts and 13 starvation losses; the staged Fox population later died out, so the late two-hunter contract correctly remained incomplete. |

The deliberate bot no longer has to auto-complete within the live horizon for the strategy contrast to pass. It must reach the compound refuge arc in a recoverable state with sufficient natural births and hunts. Exact completion reachability is verified by the deterministic progression suite.

## Implementation

- `config/game_config.gd` defines per-checkpoint `criteria` arrays, hunt-first sequences, stronger population targets, prey ratios, and holds.
- `game/run_director.gd` evaluates compound criteria, fresh newborn identity, separated birth zones, distinct living hunters, prey ratios, health, trend, and ordered cycles.
- Nursery evidence uses bounded, route-aware local nuclei, ignores unrelated wanderers, honors the configured center separation, and can count targets above two.
- Safe-haven evidence is sampled at most twice per simulation second during live play to avoid repeating route-heavy spatial work every fixed tick. Tests disable that cache when exact immediate evaluation is required.
- `ui/game_hud.gd` groups structured goals into literal actions, live conditions, and the final hold, then chooses guidance from the first unmet ecological criterion.
- `tests/progression_runner.gd` covers every compound contract, false positive, reward, Critical state, UI mapping, and full completion.

## Validation

- Compound progression/failure/UI suite: 31 passed.
- Terrain/reachability suite: 13 passed.
- Core behavior suite: 34 passed; all five ecological scenarios passed.
- Dump strategy: correctly stalled at the first separated-geography checkpoint.
- Deliberate strategy: progressed into the late compound arc without automatic completion.
- Predator-overstock strategy: remained incomplete after destabilizing both sides of the food web.
