# Biome Bloome V0.5 compound progression report

## Outcome

V0.5 uses ten meadow beats. Five focused rabbit checkpoints build the colony in deliberate steps before the five-checkpoint predator arc begins.

The progression layer accepts multiple criteria per checkpoint and exposes every live condition to the HUD. Event evidence is fresh to the current checkpoint; living-state evidence must remain true through the configured hold.

## Checkpoint arc

| # | Checkpoint | Compound proof |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | A Colony Gathers | Four living rabbits hold for four seconds. |
| 2 | New Arrivals | Six living rabbits, including two fresh natural births. |
| 3 | Young Foragers | Eight living rabbits, with two meadow-born rabbits mature enough to feed. |
| 4 | Life Across the Meadow | Ten living rabbits and fresh births in three separated areas. |
| 5 | A Nursery Network | Twelve living rabbits and three simultaneous nurseries of at least three rabbits near usable food. |
| 6 | Hunt and Recover | A fox kill followed by a rabbit birth, with viable populations. |
| 7 | Predator–Prey Rhythm | Fox kill → rabbit birth → fox kill, with species-specific hunger and quantitative rabbit-loss status. |
| 8 | Nurseries Under Pressure | Two separated viable nurseries survive `hunt → birth` and a 12-second all-goals hold. |
| 9 | Two Hunting Foxes | Three nurseries, kill → birth → kill, two distinct living hunters, and four rabbits per fox survive a 14-second hold. |
| 10 | Living Ecosystem | Three nurseries, two hunters, births in separated zones, four rabbits per fox, and `hunt → birth → hunt → birth → hunt` survive a 20-second hold. |

Extra births or hunts do not invalidate ordered evidence. An unfinished sequence expires at the configured window; a completed sequence stays latched while the living conditions settle.

## Playtest-led tuning

The opening targets are intentionally cumulative without duplicating the same proof: population, births, young feeding, distributed births, then live nurseries. The 4 → 6 → 8 → 10 → 12 population ramp prevents passive evidence from advancing a checkpoint after losses, while three birth areas remain achievable inside the opening meadow. Four incremental checkpoint expansions provide room for the three three-rabbit nursery groups before checkpoint 5, and the Objective Lens makes recognized groups visible rather than leaving the spatial test hidden.

The strategy bot was improved instead of weakening the game. It now:

- establishes six founders across three habitats;
- searches for productive terrain before placing plants;
- prioritizes recovery supplies below the rabbit reserve;
- delays a second fox until the ecosystem has rebuilt;
- can spend additional forage inventory during a low-population recovery.

Manual playtesting then exposed a non-monotonic connected-component rule: extra Rabbits could chain healthy nurseries together, and one unfed wanderer could invalidate the resulting oversized component. The internal `safe_havens` evidence contract now recognizes bounded local nursery nuclei with their own forage. Both the opening network and expanded late-game meadow support three distinct groups.

The long strategy harness caps dense dumping at 16 rabbits, deliberate play at 20, the checkpoint-reactive opening at 24, and Foxes at 10 to keep route-aware headless comparisons practical. These ceilings do not change the live game configuration; they provide enough bounded headroom to test the three-nursery hold as the gradually expanding terrain gives Rabbits more room to roam.

Observed deterministic-seed outcomes:

| Strategy | Horizon/outcome | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Dump everything | Stalled at Life Across the Meadow at 360 seconds | 16 rabbits and 27 natural births produced only two separated birthplaces. Quantity still cannot bypass geography. |
| Deliberate nursery-network, 20-rabbit ceiling | Completed A Nursery Network at 172.0 seconds and reached Predator–Prey Rhythm by 220 | The restored three-nursery checkpoint took 112.3 seconds: difficult, but attainable without relaxing its 10-second hold. |
| Checkpoint-reactive, 24-rabbit ceiling | Completed A Nursery Network at 101.2 seconds and reached Living Ecosystem by 321.8 | Starting with the compact four-rabbit colony taught by checkpoint 1, it spread only when checkpoint 4 requested birthplaces; checkpoint 5 then took 30.9 seconds. |
| Predator overstock, 20-rabbit ceiling | Reached Predators Find Their Place at 242.5 seconds; still playing there at 300 | Six Foxes reduced the prey ratio below target and left only one nursery, so aggressive stocking remained strategically self-defeating. |

The deliberate bot no longer has to auto-complete within the live horizon for the strategy contrast to pass. It must reach the compound nursery arc in a recoverable state with sufficient natural births and hunts. Exact completion reachability is verified by the deterministic progression suite.

## Implementation

- `config/game_config.gd` defines per-checkpoint `criteria` arrays, hunt-first sequences, stronger population targets, prey ratios, and holds.
- `game/run_director.gd` evaluates compound criteria, fresh newborn identity, separated birth zones, distinct living hunters, prey ratios, health, trend, and ordered cycles.
- Nursery evidence uses bounded, route-aware local nuclei, ignores unrelated wanderers, honors the configured center separation, and can count targets above two.
- Nursery evidence is sampled at most twice per simulation second during live play to avoid repeating route-heavy spatial work every fixed tick. Tests disable that cache when exact immediate evaluation is required.
- `ui/game_hud.gd` groups structured goals into literal actions, live conditions, and the final hold, then chooses guidance from the first unmet ecological criterion.
- `tests/progression_runner.gd` covers every compound contract, false positive, reward, Critical state, UI mapping, and full completion.

## Validation

- Compound progression/failure/UI suite: 31 passed.
- Terrain/reachability suite: 13 passed.
- Core behavior suite: 34 passed; all five ecological scenarios passed.
- Dump strategy: correctly stalled at the first separated-geography checkpoint.
- Deliberate strategy: progressed into the late compound arc without automatic completion.
- Predator-overstock strategy: remained incomplete after destabilizing both sides of the food web.
