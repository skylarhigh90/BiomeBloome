# Gameplay audit follow-up — 19 September 2026

This pass improves the rabbit activity model and repairs two progression bottlenecks identified while replaying the [September 17 audit](audits/2026-09-17/GAMEPLAY_SIMULATION_AUDIT.md). It is a tested first implementation of that audit's priorities, not a claim that its predator ecology work is finished.

## What changed

- **Feeding is a stationary activity.** Rabbits choose spaced, reachable positions around a patch, slow on approach, face the food, and stop moving while consuming it. An animal already beside uncrowded food can eat immediately. Transplanting the plant invalidates the old feeding position.
- **Rest is stationary too.** Rabbits rest, watch, and make brief position changes near home. They yield space when crowded. Hunger and danger interrupt these activities; a feeding rabbit escapes on the next simulation step.
- **Hops move the entire animal.** Fixed-step gait phase drives both forward impulses and rendered lift/compression. Shadows stay grounded with a consistent light direction. Facing persists through stops; feeding has a small chewing pose and resting has tucked ears/closed eyes. Gait follows pause and simulation speed.
- **Food intake deliberately takes longer.** Intake is 1.2 biomass/second instead of 4.5; hunger rate and food value are unchanged. Minimum usable bite changes from 0.45 to 0.12. This is an ecological tuning change, not just animation. The field note now counts feeding visits, including interrupted visits, instead of labelling every bite tick a meal.
- **Nursery formation is actionable.** Rabbit placement previews count reachable companions. Completing Two Lasting Homes gives three placeable rabbits and two carrot patches for the third nursery, alongside the existing Transplant charge. These are inventory rewards: the player chooses where they go.
- **The finale allows losses.** A Nursery Network still requires three live nurseries. Living Balance requires two surviving nurseries, preserving the third as a buffer when predators scatter a group. Three recent births, three recent hunts, twelve rabbits, two foxes, prey ratio, hunger requirements, and the confirmation hold remain.
- **Sandbox HUD refresh is fixed.** An explicitly typed observation-goal array replaces the dynamic array call that threw an error after completion. The regression test now checks that old checkpoint rows actually disappear, not just that the title changes.
- **Plant updates reuse cached habitat suitability.** The former `Dictionary.get` fallback recalculated terrain suitability even when the cached value existed. Cache invalidation on Transplant remains intact.
- **Empty-water route checks return immediately.** On a map without a stream, the route predicate no longer samples intermediate points whose water depth is always zero. This repaired a failing 150-rabbit/30-fox smoke timing check without changing its budget or bypassing stream/ford routing.

## Matched measurements

The baseline is a copy of the working tree taken before production edits, including the user's existing uncommitted work. Both versions use the same compact-colony harness, normal lifespan/reproduction/population caps, no intervention, 120 simulation seconds, and seeds 240817, 9327, 401, 9031 and 17117. Speeds use actual displacement, not a velocity label. Values below are medians of the five per-seed measurements.

| Measurement | Before | Revised |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Speed while eating | 32.7 world units/s | 0 |
| Speed while resting (`loaf`) | 4.1 world units/s | 0 |
| Completed eating-bout duration | 0.4 s | 1.4 s |
| Samples with another rabbit within 18 units | 35.4% | 3.0% |

Every seed improves on these movement/spacing measures. Revised overlap ranges from 1.6% to 15.1%, so crowding is reduced, not eliminated. All sampled eating/resting states are stationary. Across these five short colony trials, baseline starvation deaths total one and revised deaths zero; births total 23 versus 20. Different feeding and movement trajectories do affect ecology, and these short results do not establish long-term stability. [Raw comparison](playtests/2026-09-19/motion-comparison.json).

## Playthrough results

The matched deliberate strategy uses the same placement/supply algorithm, seed 240817, fixed 0.1-second steps and shipped population caps of 400 rabbits/80 foxes. The old harness's silent 20-rabbit/10-fox limits were removed for **both** sides of this comparison. Supplies and deliberate reinforcement are allowed, as in normal play; this is a progression test, not a no-intervention ecological trial.

| Result | Before | Revised |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outcome | Nursery Network still incomplete at 1,200 s | All six acts complete at 546.8 s |
| Sandbox continuation | Not reached | Simulation continues |
| Natural rabbit births | 55 | 48 |
| Successful hunts | 0; foxes not unlocked | 7 |
| Rabbit starvation deaths | 0 | 4 |
| Final rabbits / foxes | 12 / 0 | 23 / 2 |

The endpoint counts span different durations and trophic stages; they are not controlled survival-rate comparisons. The revised run claims six supplies and places four foxes over time, including replacements. Its peak rabbit population is 25, far below the hard cap. [Baseline](playtests/2026-09-19/before-deliberate.json), [revised run](playtests/2026-09-19/revised-deliberate.json).

The six completion times are **12.5, 118.7, 134.7, 157.0, 210.2 and 546.8 seconds**. The last act still takes 336.6 seconds in this strategy; it is reachable, but its pacing remains a useful target for human playtesting.

Strategy contrast remains: [dense dumping](playtests/2026-09-19/dump.json) reaches Two Lasting Homes and is still there at 360 seconds. A [companion-guided strategy on seed 9327](playtests/2026-09-19/revised-guided-9327.json) completes at 348.4 seconds, with 20 rabbits, two foxes and sandbox continuation. This second strategy uses the placement preview to reinforce underpopulated sites; it is not a matched before/after comparison or proof that every strategy/seed completes.

Intermediate failures informed the changes and are retained in the evidence folder. Motion changes alone still stalled at the nursery network. The starter bundle unlocked predators, but retaining all three nurseries in the final act created another long stall. A strategy that distributes founders using the new companion preview also showed that early predator introduction can cause a real collapse; faster early progress is not automatically better husbandry. No objectives are skipped or completed by injected birth/hunt events in the strategy tests.

## What this establishes—and what remains

There is measurable improvement in action readability, spacing and progression reachability, backed by rendered inspection and automated play. Whether people find the result more enjoyable still needs independent human playtesting.

Predator mate rendezvous, bounded perception/lost-contact search, cover concealment, multi-generation predator persistence, vegetation succession and representative performance budgets remain open audit work. Supplies replacing foxes are not evidence of a self-replacing predator lineage. This pass does not claim to resolve those systems or justify adding another species yet.
