# Future ideas (deliberately not in V0.1)

- Save/load for long-running sandbox ecosystems.
- Richer terrain and environmental pressures only after the placement/observation loop proves satisfying.
- Optional animal relocation or undo only if playtesting shows irreversible placement prevents useful experimentation.
- Additional species only after rabbits, foxes, grass, and berry bushes produce understandable local stories on their own.

V0.1 intentionally excludes hazards, weather, seasons, disease, currency, shops, progression trees, accounts, multiplayer, and rescue/game-over systems.
