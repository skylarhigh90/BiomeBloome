# Biome Bloome V0.3 implementation report

## Summary

V0.3 replaces the three broad V0.2 milestones with eight smaller ecological checkpoints. Progression is now event- and identity-driven: the director knows which rabbits were placed or born, which individual animals fed, which fox completed a hunt, and whether two genuinely separated rabbit refuges exist. The normal HUD presents qualitative coaching only; exact evaluator state remains available in F3 debug output.

The world remains continuous through all progression. Major checkpoints expand it from 360 to 505, 650, and 795 without resetting living entities.

## Important implementation files

- `config/game_config.gd`: checkpoint order, hidden tuning, rewards, supplies, Critical settings, and targeted fox balance.
- `simulation/ecosystem_simulation.gd`: identity-bearing feeding and successful-predation events.
- `game/run_director.gd`: ID-driven evaluators, spatial refuge evidence, cycle latching, trend guards, Critical/Game Over, and debug output.
- `game/game_systems.gd`: connects simulation evidence to progression and applies checkpoint rewards.
- `ui/game_hud.gd`: qualitative objective card, next-beat teasers, minor/major feedback tiers, and final overlay.
- `tests/progression_runner.gd`: checkpoint, identity, spatial false-positive, reward, UI, final-cycle, and failure coverage.
- `tests/playtest_runner.gd`: normal-tuning dump, deliberate two-refuge, and predator-overstock strategy runs with ID-keyed timing.

## Balance and pacing adjustments

- Kept the supply interval at 90 simulation seconds and retained exactly three pools.
- Shifted Living supplies toward plant support; only one Living bundle directly tops up rabbits.
- Added small fox staging grants at major checkpoints 5 and 7 to reduce late-run RNG gating after an unlucky predator death.
- Improved fox reliability for the longer arc: earlier hunting, wider prey detection, faster capture, slightly stronger meals, slower hunger, and a longer starvation runway.
- Tuned the spatial hold to normal rabbit wandering. A connected stretched colony still fails, and a transient split still resets; a deliberately formed two-refuge pattern can now survive the hidden hold reliably.
- Treats starvation as severe when it affects a meaningful share of a population, allowing isolated stragglers without permitting a collapsing web to complete the final checkpoint.
- Extra births or hunts no longer erase correct final-cycle progress; only the next required event advances it, the overall evidence window still expires, and completion remains latched during the final hold.

## Validation results

- Behavior suite: 29 passed.
- Ecology scenarios: 5 passed, including poor placement, predator introduction, predator overstock collapse, and spatial outcome differences.
- V0.3 progression/failure suite: 24 passed.
- Visual smoke and full project launch: passed.
- Rendered 1280×800 review: objective copy, population strip, world readability, two-refuge layout, and hidden-mechanics policy inspected with the real OpenGL renderer.

Normal-tuning strategy outcomes:

| Strategy | Outcome | Stopping point/time | Useful signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dump everything | Stalled | `two_safe_havens` at 360 simulated seconds | One dense connected colony does not satisfy the spatial checkpoint |
| Deliberate two-refuge | Completed | 481.3 simulated seconds | All eight checkpoints, 94 natural births, 6 successful hunts, 5 supplies, sandbox continuation |
| Predator overstock | Game Over | `life_returns` at 199.7 simulated seconds | Four foxes reduced the rabbit lineage below recovery capacity |

The longest deliberate checkpoint wait was 185 simulated seconds at Two Safe Havens (about 62 seconds at 3×). The final checkpoint took 77.6 simulated seconds after checkpoint 7. Exact timings are intentionally absent from the normal HUD.

## Remaining risks and tradeoffs

- The spatial checkpoint is intentionally tolerant of ordinary wandering, so its hidden hold is shorter than other milestones. False positives are constrained by connected-component grouping, food reachability, minimum group size, predator-scale separation, and a nonzero hold, but broader player testing should revisit this threshold.
- Large rabbit booms are computationally expensive and can lengthen headless strategy tests; the dump strategy is intentionally capped to a shorter observation window.
- Late-run outcomes remain emergent. A poorly supplied or badly staged ecosystem can still lose foxes or enter Critical; staging rewards reduce bad luck without guaranteeing success.
- The normal HUD does not reveal exact progress, by design. Qualitative coaching therefore has to carry more instructional weight and should be watched in novice playtests.

## Future ideas not implemented

No new species, biome systems, weather, seasons, disease, currency, shops, progression trees, save/load, accounts, multiplayer, relocation, or undo were added. Those remain post-V0.3 work and should be considered only after the new ecological arc proves durable in broader playtesting.
